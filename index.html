Common Phishing Examples

Learn to recognize suspicious messages before sharing personal information.

📧 Fake Bank Email

"Your account has been suspended. Verify your identity immediately!"

Warning: Unexpected urgency and requests for account information.

🌐 Fake Login Website

Attackers may create websites that imitate trusted login pages.

Warning: Misspelled domains and unfamiliar website addresses.

📱 Smishing Attack

"Your package delivery failed. Update your address using this link."

Warning: Unexpected SMS links and requests for payment information.

👥 Social Engineering

Someone pretends to be IT support and asks for your verification code.

Warning: Never share passwords or one-time authentication codes.

How to Stay Protected

  • Verify suspicious messages through official channels.
  • Check website addresses carefully.
  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  • Never share passwords or verification codes.
  • Keep browsers and software updated.
  • Report suspicious messages to the relevant organization.
